# YouTube auto-caption "transcripts" are mostly noise here

*From LEARNINGS.md, section "Squeezing copyright signal out of YouTube (2026-08-01)".*

**YouTube auto-caption "transcripts" are mostly noise here.** 315 caption files exist, but only **84 are Armenian** — the rest are YouTube misdetecting the language (92 Turkish, 49 English, 24 Russian, 20 Romanian, 15 Hindi, ...) and transcribing Armenian speech as `[Muzică]` / `[Müzik]` / `[संगीत]` plus fragments. Real coverage is 84/702 = 12%, not the 45% the file count suggests; another 388 videos have no captions at all.
