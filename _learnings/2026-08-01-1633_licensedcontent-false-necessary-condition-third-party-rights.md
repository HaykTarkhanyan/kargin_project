# `licensedContent = False` is a NECESSARY condition for third-party rights in this archive

*From LEARNINGS.md, section "Squeezing copyright signal out of YouTube (2026-08-01)".*

**`licensedContent = False` is a NECESSARY condition for third-party rights in this archive** (not merely correlated). It means the channel owner did *not* successfully assert partner ownership. Two independent indicators — the 17 videos with a public "Suggested by SME/UMG" music panel, and the 35 with a `regionRestriction` — land on `False` **52 times out of 52**, never once on `True`, against a 63% `True` base rate (p ~ 1e-11). Practical payoff: music-identification work only needs to cover the 260 `False` videos, a 63% cut. Treat as a very strong prior, not a guarantee.
