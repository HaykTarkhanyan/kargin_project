# The annotation topic taxonomy is the wrong granularity for a "collection", and mention-matching is worse

*From LEARNINGS.md, section "2026-09-12 — collections, and four things that looked like bugs but were not".*

**The annotation topic taxonomy is the wrong granularity for a "collection", and mention-matching is worse.** `topics` tags 328 of 702 sketches `family` and 136 `money`: that is a filter, and the site already has filters. Searching prose for a theme is no better on its own — matching `doctor`, `patient` or the `medical` topic proposed **109** sketches, because a doctor gets mentioned in passing everywhere. Requiring a medical *setting* (hospital, clinic, dentist, examination room) cut it to 44 real members. Of 286 proposals across four themes, 80 memberships survived review.
